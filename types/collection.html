I. Sixteen Golden Ratio Constructions

Chapter 1 of The Glorious Golden Ratio (posamentier-2012) surveys sixteen distinct Euclidean and Mascheroni constructions that produce the golden ratio \(\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}\). Each entry below is modeled from scratch in geometor.posamentier.constructions using 100% exact SymPy coordinates, verified against verify_golden_ratio(), and rendered as an interactive .euclid-graphic SVG.

II. Golden Figures, Facts & Appearances

Chapters 2 through 7 of The Glorious Golden Ratio (posamentier-2012) extend the golden section from compass-and-straightedge constructions into historical geometry, Fibonacci–Lucas number theory, planar and polyhedral figures, unexpected combinatorial and geometric appearances, botanical phyllotaxis, and self-similar fractals.

III. Triangle & Circle Geometry Gems

Alongside The Glorious Golden Ratio, Alfred S. Posamentier’s geometry monographs (Secrets of Triangles, Geometric Gems) highlight classical triangle and circle theorems. This collection formalizes those theorems in original GEOMETOR prose with exact symbolic verifiers and cross-links to elements and geometor/etc.
