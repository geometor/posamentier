Exact Fibonacci (\(F_n\)) and Lucas (\(L_n\)) Formulas¶

Binet’s Formula (\(F_n\))

\(F_n = \frac{\phi^n - \psi^n}{\sqrt{5}}\)

verify_binet_fibonacci()

Binet’s Formula (\(L_n\))

\(L_n = \phi^n + \psi^n = F_{n-1} + F_{n+1}\)

verify_binet_lucas()

Cassini’s Identity

\(F_{n+1} F_{n-1} - F_n^2 = (-1)^n\)

verify_cassini_identity()

Catalan’s Identity

\(F_n^2 - F_{n-r} F_{n+r} = (-1)^{n-r} F_r^2\)

Generalizes Cassini (\(r=1\))

d’Ocagne’s Identity

\(F_m F_{n+1} - F_{m+1} F_n = (-1)^n F_{m-n}\)

Addition determinant

Vajda’s Identity

\(F_{n+i} F_{n+j} - F_n F_{n+i+j} = (-1)^n F_i F_j\)

Unifies Catalan & d’Ocagne

Sum of First \(n\) Terms

\(\sum_{k=1}^{n} F_k = F_{n+2} - 1, \qquad \sum_{k=1}^{n} F_k^2 = F_n F_{n+1}\)

Telescoping & tiling proof

Millin Series

\(\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{F_{2^k}} = \frac{7 - \sqrt{5}}{2} = 4 - \phi\)

Fermat-indexed reciprocals

Fibonacci–Pythagorean Triples

\(\left(F_n F_{n+3},\; 2 F_{n+1} F_{n+2},\; F_{n+1}^2 + F_{n+2}^2\right)\)

Satisfies \(a^2 + b^2 = c^2\)
