IV. Appendix of Formulas¶

This dedicated Appendix of Formulas consolidates the exact symbolic identities verified in geometor.posamentier.formulas, organized across four reference tables:

  1. Golden Ratio (:math:`phi`): Algebraic, radical, continued-fraction, power-linearization, and pentagonal trigonometric/spread identities.

  2. Fibonacci & Lucas Sequences: Binet, Cassini, d’Ocagne, Catalan, Vajda, summation laws, Millin series, and Pythagorean triple generators.

  3. Triangle Centers, Areas & Circles: Heron, inradius, circumradius, exradii, Euler’s \(OI^2 = R(R-2r)\), Nine-Point Circle, medians, angle bisectors, and barycentric/trilinear coordinates cross-linked to geometor/etc.

  4. Classical Geometric Theorems: Ceva, Menelaus, Stewart, Ptolemy, Brahmagupta, Parameshvara, Morley, Kepler’s triangle, and Golden Cuboid / Platonic solid invariants.

Appendix A · Golden Ratio Formulas (\phi)

Exact algebraic, radical, continued-fraction, power-linearization, and pentagonal trigonometric/spread formulas for phi.

Appendix B · Fibonacci & Lucas Identities

Exact Binet, Lucas, Cassini, d’Ocagne, Catalan, Vajda, summation, Millin series, and Fibonacci-Pythagorean formulas.

Appendix C · Triangle Centers, Areas & Circles

Exact formulas for Heron area, inradius, circumradius, exradii, Euler OI^2, Nine-Point Circle, medians, angle bisectors, and ETC coordinates.

Appendix D · Classical Geometric Theorems & Solids

Exact formulas for Ceva, Menelaus, Stewart, Ptolemy, Brahmagupta, Parameshvara, Morley, Kepler’s triangle, and the Golden Cuboid.
