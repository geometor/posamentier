Appendix C · Triangle Centers, Areas & Circles¶

Cross-Links: Incenter X(1) · Centroid X(2) · Circumcenter X(3) · Orthocenter X(4) · Nine-Point Center X(5)

Let \(\triangle ABC\) have side lengths \(a, b, c\), semiperimeter \(s = \frac{a+b+c}{2}\), and area \(K\).

Exact Triangle & Circle Formulas¶

Quantity

Exact Formula

Verifier / Reference

Heron’s Area

\(K = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}\)

verify_heron_and_inradius()

Inradius (\(r\)) & Exradii (\(r_a\))

\(r = \frac{K}{s}, \qquad r_a = \frac{K}{s - a}\)

\(K = \sqrt{r \, r_a \, r_b \, r_c}\)

Circumradius (\(R\))

\(R = \frac{abc}{4K} = \frac{a}{2\sin A}\)

Euclid IV.5

Nine-Point Circle Radius (\(R_9\))

\(R_9 = \frac{R}{2}\)

Tangent to incircle & excircles

Euler’s \(OI^2\) Formula

\(OI^2 = R(R - 2r)\)

verify_euler_oi_distance()

Euler Line Ratios

\(OG : GN_9 : N_9 H = 2 : 1 : 3, \qquad OH = 3\,OG\)

\(O = X(3), G = X(2), N_9 = X(5), H = X(4)\)

Median Length (\(m_a\))

\(m_a = \frac{1}{2}\sqrt{2b^2 + 2c^2 - a^2}\)

\(m_a^2 + m_b^2 + m_c^2 = \frac{3}{4}(a^2+b^2+c^2)\)

Angle Bisector Length (\(w_a\))

\(w_a = \frac{2bc}{b+c}\cos\frac{A}{2} = \sqrt{bc\left(1 - \frac{a^2}{(b+c)^2}\right)}\)

Euclid VI.3

Barycentric Coordinates

\(X(1) = (a:b:c), \; X(2) = (1:1:1), \; X(3) = (a^2(b^2+c^2-a^2):\dots)\)
