POS-1.03 · 1x3 Three-Square Grid Angle Bisector Section¶

Field: \(Q(phi)\) | Classification: Net-New | Concordance: Cousin of GC-03.04 · Euclid VI.3

On a unit-square grid forming right triangle ABC with legs \(|AB|=1\) and \(|AC|=2\), the interior angle bisector of B cuts the unit square side AD at P in the golden ratio \(|AP|/|PD| = \phi\).

Constructive Steps¶

step Construct two stacked unit squares ABED and DEFC along \([0,1]\times[0,2]\).
step Join hypotenuse BC of length \(\sqrt{5}\).
step Construct the angle bisector BP of \(\angle ABC\), meeting AD at P.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

By Euclid VI.3, \(|AP|/|PC| = |AB|/|BC| = 1/\sqrt{5}\). Since \(|AC|=2\), \(|AP| = 2/(1+\sqrt{5}) = 1/\phi\), while \(|PD| = 1 - 1/\phi = 1/\phi^2\), so \(|AP|/|PD| = \phi\).

Symbolic Certificate¶

Invariant

Exact Symbolic Value

Golden Ratio Segment Pair

AP / PD \(= \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} = \phi\)
