I. Sixteen Golden Ratio Constructions¶

Chapter 1 of The Glorious Golden Ratio (posamentier-2012) surveys sixteen distinct Euclidean and Mascheroni constructions that produce the golden ratio \(\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}\). Each entry below is modeled from scratch in geometor.posamentier.constructions using 100% exact SymPy coordinates, verified against verify_golden_ratio(), and rendered as an interactive .euclid-graphic SVG.

POS-1.01 · Classical Square Side-Midpoint Arc Extension

Given segment AB, erect square ABCD, bisect side AD at M, transfer MB onto ray DA beyond A at E, and swing AE onto AB at G so that \(|AG|/|GB| = \phi\).

POS-1.02 · 1:2:sqrt(5) Right-Triangle Hypotenuse Arc Transfer

In a \(1:2:\sqrt{5}\) right triangle ABC with right angle at B, subtract leg BC from hypotenuse AC via circle cCB at D, and transfer AD onto AB at G so that \(|AG|/|GB| = \phi\).

POS-1.03 · 1x3 Three-Square Grid Angle Bisector Section

On a unit-square grid forming right triangle ABC with legs \(|AB|=1\) and \(|AC|=2\), the interior angle bisector of B cuts the unit square side AD at P in the golden ratio \(|AP|/|PD| = \phi\).

POS-1.04 · 1x2 Double-Square Shared-Midpoint Diagonal Circle

In a \(1\times 2\) rectangle ABCD with center O, the inscribed circle cOM of radius \(1/2\) cuts diagonal AC at P and Q so that \(|PQ|/|AP| = \phi\).

POS-1.05 · Unit Square Extended-Side Semicircle Through Opposite Vertex

In unit square ABCD, the semicircle centered at midpoint M of AB passing through C and D cuts ray AB beyond B at F so that \(|AB|/|BF| = \phi\).

POS-1.06 · George Odom’s Inscribed Equilateral Triangle Midline Chord

In equilateral triangle ABC inscribed in circle cO, the line through midpoints M and N of AB and AC meets cO at P so that \(|MN|/|NP| = \phi\).

POS-1.07 · 2:sqrt(5):sqrt(5) Isosceles Triangle Incircle Altitude Section

In an isosceles triangle ABC of base \(|AB|=2\) and legs \(\sqrt{5}\), the incircle cI of radius \(1/\phi\) cuts the altitude OC at T so that \(|OT|/|TC| = \phi\).

POS-1.08 · Unit Square Diagonal Circle & Equilateral Hybrid Locus

Erect equilateral triangle ABE on side AB of unit square ABCD. A circle cE centered at E with radius equal to square diagonal AC (\(\sqrt{2}\)) cuts ray AB at F so that \(|AB|/|BF| = \phi\).

POS-1.09 · Circle Unit Chord & Equal External Tangent Secant Cut

From external point P, draw tangent PT of length \(1\) to circle cO of diameter \(1\). Secant PO cuts cO at A and B so that \(|AB|/|PA| = \phi\).

POS-1.10 · Huntley’s 3-4-5 Right-Triangle Angle Bisector Circle

In a \(3\text{–}4\text{–}5\) right triangle ABC (\(|AC|=3, |AB|=4\)), the angle bisector of C meets AB at E. Circle cE through A cuts line CE at P and Q so that \(|PQ|/|CP| = \phi\).

POS-1.11 · Three Concentric Circles (r = 1, 2, 4) Secant-Tangent Transversal

Three concentric circles c1, c2, c3 have radii \(1, 2, 4\). A tangent to c1 at T cuts c2 at AB and c3 at D so that \(|AB|/|BD| = \phi\).

POS-1.12 · Three Adjacent Congruent Unit Circles Outer Secant

Three unit circles c1, c2, c3 are collinearly tangent. The line joining the bottom of c1 (A) to the top of c3 (B) cuts c2 at M and N so that \(|MN|/|NB| = \phi\).

POS-1.13 · Three Mutually Tangent Circles Inscribed in a Semicircle

Three mutually tangent unit circles cC1, cC0, cC2 sit on diameter AB inside semicircle cO. The radius OB and center-offset OT2 satisfy \(|OT2|/|T2B| = \phi\).

POS-1.14 · Hans Walser’s Coordinate-Grid Radical Circles (r = 1 and r = 3)

On an integer coordinate grid, circle c1 centered at O1 \(=(0,0)\) of radius \(1\) and circle c2 centered at O2 \(=(2,1)\) of radius \(3\) define a center-line O1O2 cutting c1 at A and B such that \(|AB|/|BO2| = \phi\).

POS-1.15 · Right-Triangle Two-Arc Radical Leg Difference

In a \(1:2:\sqrt{5}\) right triangle ABC (\(|AB|=1, |BC|=2\)), transfer AB onto hypotenuse AC at E, and transfer CE onto leg BC at F so that \(|CF|/|FB| = \phi\).

POS-1.16 · Mascheroni / Hofstetter Compass-Only Circle Construction

Using strictly five compass circles and zero straightedge lines, Hofstetter constructs X collinear with C and D such that \(|CD|/|DX| = \phi\).
