POS-1.10 · Huntley’s 3-4-5 Right-Triangle Angle Bisector Circle¶

Field: \(Q(phi)\) | Classification: Equivalent | Concordance: GC-04.01 · HUNT-03.01 · H. E. Huntley · Euclid VI.3

In a \(3\text{–}4\text{–}5\) right triangle ABC (\(|AC|=3, |AB|=4\)), the angle bisector of C meets AB at E. Circle cE through A cuts line CE at P and Q so that \(|PQ|/|CP| = \phi\).

Constructive Steps¶

step Construct \(3\text{–}4\text{–}5\) right triangle ABC with A \(=(0,0)\), B \(=(4,0)\), C \(=(0,3)\).
step Draw the angle bisector of C, meeting AB at E \(=(3/2, 0)\).
step Draw circle cE centered at E through A, cutting line CE at P and Q.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

By Euclid VI.3, \(|AE|/|EB| = 3/5\), so \(|AE| = 3/2\) and \(|CE| = \frac{3}{2}\sqrt{5}\). Thus \(|CP| = \frac{3}{2}(\sqrt{5}-1) = 3/\phi\) and diameter \(|PQ| = 3\), giving \(|PQ|/|CP| = \phi\).

Symbolic Certificate¶

Invariant

Exact Symbolic Value

Golden Ratio Segment Pair

PQ / CP \(= \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} = \phi\)
