POS-1.02 · 1:2:sqrt(5) Right-Triangle Hypotenuse Arc Transfer¶

Field: \(Q(phi)\) | Classification: Equivalent | Concordance: GC-01.02 · KNOTT-1.1 · HUNT-02.02 · Euclid VI.30

In a \(1:2:\sqrt{5}\) right triangle ABC with right angle at B, subtract leg BC from hypotenuse AC via circle cCB at D, and transfer AD onto AB at G so that \(|AG|/|GB| = \phi\).

Constructive Steps¶

step Construct right triangle ABC with A \(=(0,0)\), B \(=(2,0)\), and C \(=(2,1)\).
step Draw circle cCB centered at C through B (radius \(1\)), cutting hypotenuse AC at D.
step Draw circle cAD centered at A through D, cutting base AB at G.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate¶

Since \(|AC| = \sqrt{5}\) and \(|CD| = 1\), \(|AD| = \sqrt{5} - 1\). Swinging \(|AD|\) onto \(|AB| = 2\) leaves \(|GB| = 3 - \sqrt{5}\), so \(|AG|/|GB| = (\sqrt{5}-1)/(3-\sqrt{5}) = \phi\).

Symbolic Certificate¶

Invariant

Exact Symbolic Value

Golden Ratio Segment Pair

AG / GB \(= \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} = \phi\)

Algebraic Number Field

\(Q(phi)\)
