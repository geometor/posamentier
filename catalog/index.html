Mathematical Catalog¶

Atomic Golden-Ratio Constructions, Theorems & Cross-Corpus Equivalences

Reference Work: The Glorious Golden Ratio (Prometheus Books, 2011/2012, ISBN 978-1-61614-423-4)
Authors: Alfred S. Posamentier & Ingmar Lehmann (Ch. 7 on Fractals by Ana Lúcia Braz Dias)
Digital Synthesis: GEOMETOR (100% Exact SymPy Algebra, geometor.model Constructions & Equivalence Triage)

This catalog extracts the atomic mathematical claims, Euclidean constructions, and algebraic identities embedded across the seven chapters and 38-page Proofs Appendix (pp. 295–332) of The Glorious Golden Ratio, pairing each fact with an original GEOMETOR enunciation, exact symbolic proof over \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\), and structural classification (Equivalent, Shade/Variant, or Net-New) against geometor/divine, geometor/knott, and geometor/huntley.

Chapter Architecture¶

  1. Ch. 1 — Defining and Constructing the Golden Ratio (pp. 13–38 · Proofs pp. 295–304): 16 numbered straightedge-and-compass and Mascheroni circle constructions (POS-1.01–POS-1.16).

  2. Ch. 2 — History of the Golden Ratio (pp. 39–48): Kepler’s \(1 : \sqrt{\phi} : \phi\) triangle, Euclid, Fibonacci, Pacioli, Ohm, Zeising, and exact-vs-approximate historical residual audit.

  3. Ch. 3 — Numerical Value & Algebraic Properties (pp. 49–80 · Proofs pp. 305–311): \(\phi^n = F_n \phi + F_{n-1}\), continued fraction [1; 1, 1, ...], nested radicals, Binet/Lucas/Cassini identities, Millin series, and \(18^\circ / 36^\circ\) radicals.

  4. Ch. 4 — Golden Geometric Figures (pp. 81–190 · Proofs pp. 312–325): Whirling-square rectangles, adjacent semicircles cutting the square diagonal in \(\phi\) (POS-4.01), golden triangles & gnomons, folded paper-strip pentagon knot (POS-4.02), Golden Ellipse, Golden Cuboid \(1 \times \phi \times \phi^2\) (POS-4.03), and icosahedron/dodecahedron coordinates.

  5. Ch. 5 — Unexpected Appearances (pp. 191–246 · Proofs pp. 326–330): Star of David hexagram \(\phi\) chord section (POS-5.01), similar triangles on reference-triangle sides, 64 = 65 Cassini dissection, Pascal shallow diagonals, and random-walk stopping probability \(1/\phi\).

  6. Ch. 6 — The Plant Kingdom (pp. 247–268): Phyllotaxis parastichies and the Golden Angle \(360^\circ / \phi^2\) (cross-linked to geometor/phyllotaxis).

  7. Ch. 7 — Fractals and the Golden Ratio (pp. 269–292 · Proofs pp. 331–332, by Ana Lúcia Braz Dias): Symmetric Golden Fractal Tree \(r = 1/\phi\) (POS-7.01) and Sierpiński-Pentagon \(r = 1/\phi^2\) (POS-7.02).
