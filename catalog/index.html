Mathematical Catalog¶

Atomic Golden-Ratio Constructions, Geometric Facts, Appendix of Formulas & Cross-Corpus Equivalences

Reference Corpus: The Glorious Golden Ratio (Prometheus Books, 2011/2012, ISBN 978-1-61614-423-4) & Triangle/Circle Geometry Treatises
Authors: Alfred S. Posamentier & Ingmar Lehmann (Ch. 7 on Fractals by :person:`Ana Lúcia Braz Dias <dias>`)
Digital Synthesis: GEOMETOR (100% Exact SymPy Algebra, geometor.model Constructions & Equivalence Triage)

This catalog synthesizes the atomic mathematical claims, Euclidean constructions, geometric facts, and algebraic identities inspired by The Glorious Golden Ratio and Posamentier’s triangle and circle geometry monographs, pairing each theorem with original GEOMETOR mathematical prose, exact symbolic formulations over \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\), calibrated .euclid-graphic vector plates, and cross-corpus links to elements and geometor/etc.

Collections¶

I. Sixteen Golden Ratio Constructions

Sixteen exact straightedge-and-compass and Mascheroni circle constructions of the Golden Ratio (POS-1.01 through POS-1.16) with calibrated .euclid-graphic SVGs.

II. Golden Figures, Facts & Appearances

Geometric facts, algebraic properties, Kepler’s triangle, golden figures, unexpected appearances, phyllotaxis, and fractals from Chapters 2–7 of The Glorious Golden Ratio.

III. Triangle & Circle Geometry Gems

Classical triangle centers, Euler line, Nine-Point Circle, Ceva, Menelaus, Stewart, Ptolemy, Brahmagupta, and Morley’s trisector theorem cross-linked to elements and etc.

IV. Appendix of Formulas

Exact symbolic SymPy and LaTeX Appendix of Formulas for the Golden Ratio, Fibonacci/Lucas identities, Triangle Centers & Circles, and Classical Geometric Theorems.

Chapter Architecture¶

  1. Ch. 1 — Defining and Constructing the Golden Ratio (pp. 13–38 · Proofs pp. 295–304): 16 numbered straightedge-and-compass and Mascheroni circle constructions (POS-1.01–POS-1.16).

  2. Ch. 2 — History of the Golden Ratio (pp. 39–48): Kepler’s \(1 : \sqrt{\phi} : \phi\) triangle, Euclid, Fibonacci, Pacioli, Ohm, Zeising, and exact-vs-approximate historical residual audit.

  3. Ch. 3 — Numerical Value & Algebraic Properties (pp. 49–80 · Proofs pp. 305–311): \(\phi^n = F_n \phi + F_{n-1}\), continued fraction [1; 1, 1, ...], nested radicals, Binet/Lucas/Cassini identities, Millin series, and \(18^\circ / 36^\circ\) radicals.

  4. Ch. 4 — Golden Geometric Figures (pp. 81–190 · Proofs pp. 312–325): Whirling-square rectangles, semicircle-on-side square section (POS-4.01), golden triangles & gnomons, folded paper-strip pentagon knot (POS-4.02), Golden Ellipse, Golden Cuboid \(1 \times \phi \times \phi^2\) (POS-4.03), and icosahedron/dodecahedron coordinates.

  5. Ch. 5 — Unexpected Appearances (pp. 191–246 · Proofs pp. 326–330): Star of David hexagram \(\phi\) chord section (POS-5.01), similar triangles on reference-triangle sides, 64 = 65 Cassini dissection, Pascal shallow diagonals, and random-walk stopping probability \(1/\phi\).

  6. Ch. 6 — The Plant Kingdom (pp. 247–268): Phyllotaxis parastichies and the Golden Angle \(360^\circ / \phi^2\) (cross-linked to geometor/phyllotaxis).

  7. Ch. 7 — Fractals and the Golden Ratio (pp. 269–292 · Proofs pp. 331–332, by Ana Lúcia Braz Dias): Symmetric Golden Fractal Tree \(r = 1/\phi\) (POS-7.01) and Sierpiński-Pentagon \(r = 1/\phi^2\) (POS-7.02).
