Triangle Centers, Euler Line & Nine-Point Circle¶

Cross-Links: Incenter X(1) · Centroid X(2) · Circumcenter X(3) · Orthocenter X(4) · Nine-Point Center X(5) · Symmedian Point X(6)

In any non-equilateral triangle ABC, the circumcenter O, centroid G, nine-point center N9, and orthocenter H are collinear on the Euler line, satisfying \(OG : GN_9 : N_9 H = 2 : 1 : 3\) and \(OH = 3 \cdot OG\).

Exact 13–14–15 Heron Witness¶

For A \(=(0,0)\), B \(=(14,0)\), C \(=(5,12)\) (sides \(a=13, b=15, c=14\), area \(K=84\), semiperimeter \(s=21\)):

  • Incenter I (X(1)): \((6, 4)\), inradius \(r = K/s = 4\) (Euclid IV.4).

  • Centroid G (X(2)): \((19/3, 4)\).

  • Circumcenter O (X(3)): \((7, 33/8)\), circumradius \(R = abc/(4K) = 65/8\) (Euclid IV.5).

  • Orthocenter H (X(4)): \((5, 15/4)\).

  • Nine-Point Center N9 (X(5)): \((6, 63/16)\), nine-point radius \(R_9 = R/2 = 65/16\).

Key Circle & Line Theorems¶

  1. Nine-Point Circle: Passes through the 3 side midpoints, the 3 altitude feet, and the 3 midpoints between H and vertices ABC.

  2. Feuerbach’s Theorem: The Nine-Point Circle is internally tangent to the incircle and externally tangent to all three excircles.

  3. Euler’s Distance Formula: \(OI^2 = R(R - 2r)\), whence \(R \ge 2r\) with equality iff \(\triangle ABC\) is equilateral (verify_euler_oi_distance()).

  4. Simson–Wallace Line: The feet of the perpendiculars dropped from a point \(P\) to the sidelines of \(\triangle ABC\) are collinear if and only if \(P\) lies on the circumcircle cO.
