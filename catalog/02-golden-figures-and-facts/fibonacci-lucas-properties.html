Numerical Value & Algebraic Properties (Ch. 3)¶

Source Locus: Chapter 3 (pp. 49–80 · Proofs pp. 305–311) · Verifier: geometor.posamentier.formulas

The golden ratio \(\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}\) is the unique positive root of \(x^2 - x - 1 = 0\). Every integer power \(\phi^n\) linearizes over \(\mathbb{Q}(\phi)\) via Fibonacci coefficients: \(\phi^n = F_n \phi + F_{n-1}\).

Key Algebraic & Number-Theoretic Facts¶

  1. Continued Fraction & Nested Radical:

    \[\phi = [1; 1, 1, 1, \dots] = 1 + \cfrac{1}{1 + \cfrac{1}{1 + \ddots}} = \sqrt{1 + \sqrt{1 + \sqrt{1 + \cdots}}}\]

    The partial convergents are successive Fibonacci ratios \(F_{n+1}/F_n\), alternating above and below \(\phi\). By Hurwitz’s theorem, \(\phi\) is the most poorly approximable irrational number (\(|\phi - p/q| > 1/(\sqrt{5}\,q^2)\)).

  2. Power Linearization & Conjugate: Let \(\psi = 1 - \phi = -1/\phi = \frac{1-\sqrt{5}}{2}\). Both \(\phi\) and \(\psi\) satisfy \(x^2 = x + 1\), whence induction yields (verify_phi_power_linearization()):

    \[\phi^n = F_n \phi + F_{n-1}, \qquad \psi^n = F_n \psi + F_{n-1}, \qquad \phi^{-n} = (-1)^n \left(F_{n-1} - F_n \psi\right).\]

  3. Binet, Lucas & Cassini Identities:

    \[F_n = \frac{\phi^n - \psi^n}{\sqrt{5}}, \qquad L_n = \phi^n + \psi^n, \qquad F_{n+1}F_{n-1} - F_n^2 = (-1)^n.\]

  4. Millin Series (Reciprocal Fermat-Indexed Fibonacci Sum):

    \[\sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{1}{F_{2^k}} = \frac{7 - \sqrt{5}}{2} = 4 - \phi.\]

  5. Exact Pentagonal Trigonometry (verify_pentagonal_trig()):

    \[\sin 18^\circ = \cos 72^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{4} = \frac{1}{2\phi}, \qquad \cos 36^\circ = \sin 54^\circ = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{4} = \frac{\phi}{2}.\]
