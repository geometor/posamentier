POS-1.13 · Three Mutually Tangent Circles Inscribed in a Semicircle
===================================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Equivalent** | **Concordance:** GC-06.07 · Descartes / Soddy Sangaku quadratic

.. enunciation::

   Three mutually tangent unit circles :g:`cC1`, :g:`cC0`, :g:`cC2` sit on diameter :g:`AB` inside semicircle :g:`cO`. The radius :g:`OB` and center-offset :g:`OT2` satisfy :math:`|OT2|/|T2B| = \phi`.

.. picture:: pos-1-13.svg
   :alt: POS-1.13 — Three Mutually Tangent Circles Inscribed in a Semicircle

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Place three unit circles :g:`cC1`, :g:`cC0`, :g:`cC2` at :math:`(-2,1), (0,1), (2,1)` tangent to diameter :g:`AB` at :g:`T1`, :g:`O`, :g:`T2`.

.. step::

   Construct outer semicircle :g:`cO` centered at :g:`O` tangent to :g:`cC1` and :g:`cC2`, meeting :g:`AB` at :g:`A` and :g:`B`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

The distance from :g:`O` to :g:`C2` is :math:`\sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5}`, so the outer radius is :math:`|OB| = \sqrt{5} + 1 = 2\phi`. With :math:`|OT2| = 2` and :math:`|T2B| = 2\phi - 2 = 2/\phi`, :math:`|OT2|/|T2B| = \phi`.
