POS-1.03 · 1x3 Three-Square Grid Angle Bisector Section
=======================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Net-New** | **Concordance:** Cousin of GC-03.04 · :ref:`Euclid VI.3 <elements:vi.3>`

.. enunciation::

   On a unit-square grid forming right triangle :g:`ABC` with legs :math:`|AB|=1` and :math:`|AC|=2`, the interior angle bisector of :g:`B` cuts the unit square side :g:`AD` at :g:`P` in the golden ratio :math:`|AP|/|PD| = \phi`.

.. picture:: pos-1-03.svg
   :alt: POS-1.03 — 1x3 Three-Square Grid Angle Bisector Section

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct two stacked unit squares :g:`ABED` and :g:`DEFC` along :math:`[0,1]\times[0,2]`.

.. step::

   Join hypotenuse :g:`BC` of length :math:`\sqrt{5}`.

.. step::

   Construct the angle bisector :g:`BP` of :math:`\angle ABC`, meeting :g:`AD` at :g:`P`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

By :ref:`Euclid VI.3 <elements:vi.3>`, :math:`|AP|/|PC| = |AB|/|BC| = 1/\sqrt{5}`. Since :math:`|AC|=2`, :math:`|AP| = 2/(1+\sqrt{5}) = 1/\phi`, while :math:`|PD| = 1 - 1/\phi = 1/\phi^2`, so :math:`|AP|/|PD| = \phi`.
