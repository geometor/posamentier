POS-1.04 · 1x2 Double-Square Shared-Midpoint Diagonal Circle
============================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Variant** | **Concordance:** GC-03.01 · KNOTT-1.2

.. enunciation::

   In a :math:`1\times 2` rectangle :g:`ABCD` with center :g:`O`, the inscribed circle :g:`cOM` of radius :math:`1/2` cuts diagonal :g:`AC` at :g:`P` and :g:`Q` so that :math:`|PQ|/|AP| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct :math:`1\times 2` rectangle :g:`ABCD` with :g:`A` :math:`=(0,0)`, :g:`B` :math:`=(2,0)`, :g:`C` :math:`=(2,1)`, :g:`D` :math:`=(0,1)`.

.. step::

   Mark center :g:`O` :math:`=(1, 1/2)` and base midpoint :g:`M` :math:`=(1,0)`, and draw circle :g:`cOM`.

.. step::

   Intersect :g:`cOM` with diagonal :g:`AC` at :g:`P` (near :g:`A`) and :g:`Q` (near :g:`C`).

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Diagonal :math:`|AC| = \sqrt{5}` and diameter :math:`|PQ| = 1`. By symmetry, :math:`|AP| = |QC| = (\sqrt{5}-1)/2 = 1/\phi`, giving :math:`|PQ|/|AP| = \phi`.
