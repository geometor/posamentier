POS-1.12 · Three Adjacent Congruent Unit Circles Outer Secant
=============================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Variant** | **Concordance:** GC-06.04 · 1:2:sqrt(5) center-line transversal

.. enunciation::

   Three unit circles :g:`c1`, :g:`c2`, :g:`c3` are collinearly tangent. The line joining the bottom of :g:`c1` (:g:`A`) to the top of :g:`c3` (:g:`B`) cuts :g:`c2` at :g:`M` and :g:`N` so that :math:`|MN|/|NB| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct unit circles :g:`c1` at :math:`(-2,0)`, :g:`c2` at :math:`(0,0)`, and :g:`c3` at :math:`(2,0)`.

.. step::

   Join :g:`A` :math:`=(-2,-1)` to :g:`B` :math:`=(2,1)` through :g:`O` :math:`=(0,0)`.

.. step::

   Mark intersections :g:`M` and :g:`N` of :g:`AB` with :g:`c2`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Since :math:`|OB| = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5}` and :math:`|ON| = 1`, we have :math:`|MN| = 2` and :math:`|NB| = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi`, so :math:`|MN|/|NB| = \phi`.
