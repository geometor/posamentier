POS-1.09 · Circle Unit Chord & Equal External Tangent Secant Cut
================================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Net-New** | **Concordance:** Dual of GC-06.01 · :ref:`Euclid III.36 <elements:iii.36>`

.. enunciation::

   From external point :g:`P`, draw tangent :g:`PT` of length :math:`1` to circle :g:`cO` of diameter :math:`1`. Secant :g:`PO` cuts :g:`cO` at :g:`A` and :g:`B` so that :math:`|AB|/|PA| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Set :g:`P` :math:`=(0,0)` and tangent point :g:`T` :math:`=(1,0)`.

.. step::

   Construct circle :g:`cO` centered at :g:`O` :math:`=(1, 1/2)` through :g:`T` (diameter :math:`1`).

.. step::

   Draw secant line :g:`PO`, cutting :g:`cO` at :g:`A` and :g:`B`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

By :ref:`Tangent–Secant Power (Euclid III.36) <elements:iii.36>`, :math:`|PA|(|PA| + |AB|) = |PT|^2 = 1`. With :math:`|AB|=1`, :math:`|PA| = 1/\phi`, so :math:`|AB|/|PA| = \phi`.
