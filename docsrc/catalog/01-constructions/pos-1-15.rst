POS-1.15 · Right-Triangle Two-Arc Radical Leg Difference
========================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Variant** | **Concordance:** GC-01.02 · HUNT-02.02

.. enunciation::

   In a :math:`1:2:\sqrt{5}` right triangle :g:`ABC` (:math:`|AB|=1, |BC|=2`), transfer :g:`AB` onto hypotenuse :g:`AC` at :g:`E`, and transfer :g:`CE` onto leg :g:`BC` at :g:`F` so that :math:`|CF|/|FB| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct right triangle :g:`ABC` with :g:`A` :math:`=(0,0)`, :g:`B` :math:`=(1,0)`, :g:`C` :math:`=(1,2)`.

.. step::

   Draw circle :g:`cAB` centered at :g:`A` through :g:`B`, cutting :g:`AC` at :g:`E`.

.. step::

   Draw circle :g:`cCE` centered at :g:`C` through :g:`E`, cutting :g:`BC` at :g:`F`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Since :math:`|AC| = \sqrt{5}` and :math:`|AE| = 1`, :math:`|CF| = |CE| = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi`, leaving :math:`|FB| = 2 - (\sqrt{5}-1) = 3 - \sqrt{5} = 2/\phi^2`. Hence :math:`|CF|/|FB| = \phi`.
