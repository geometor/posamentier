======================================
I. Sixteen Golden Ratio Constructions
======================================

Chapter 1 of *The Glorious Golden Ratio* (:cite:`posamentier-2012`) surveys sixteen distinct Euclidean and Mascheroni constructions that produce the golden ratio :math:`\phi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}`. Each entry below is modeled from scratch in :mod:`geometor.posamentier.constructions` using 100% exact ``SymPy`` coordinates, verified against :func:`~geometor.posamentier.constructions.verify_golden_ratio`, and rendered as an interactive ``.euclid-graphic`` SVG.
