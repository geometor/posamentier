POS-1.07 · 2:sqrt(5):sqrt(5) Isosceles Triangle Incircle Altitude Section
=========================================================================

.. enunciation::

   In an isosceles triangle :g:`ABC` of base :math:`|AB|=2` and legs :math:`\sqrt{5}`, the incircle :g:`cI` of radius :math:`1/\phi` cuts the altitude :g:`OC` at :g:`T` so that :math:`|OT|/|TC| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct isosceles :g:`ABC` with :g:`A` :math:`=(-1,0)`, :g:`B` :math:`=(1,0)`, :g:`C` :math:`=(0,2)`, and base midpoint :g:`O` :math:`=(0,0)`.

.. step::

.. step::

   Mark the upper intersection :g:`T` of :g:`cI` with altitude :g:`OC`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Semiperimeter :math:`s = 1 + \sqrt{5} = 2\phi` and area :math:`K = 2`, so inradius :math:`r = K/s = 1/\phi`. Thus :math:`|OT| = 2/\phi` and :math:`|TC| = 2 - 2/\phi = 2/\phi^2`, giving :math:`|OT|/|TC| = \phi`.
