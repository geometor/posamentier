src.geometor.posamentier.constructions¶

Exact symbolic geometor.model constructions for geometor.posamentier.

Every construction uses 100% exact SymPy coordinates over Q(phi) or cyclotomic radical extensions with Model(..., auto_intersect=False) and zero floating-point approximations.
