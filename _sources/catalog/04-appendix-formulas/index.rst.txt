========================
IV. Appendix of Formulas
========================

This dedicated **Appendix of Formulas** consolidates the exact symbolic identities verified in :mod:`geometor.posamentier.formulas`, organized across four reference tables:

1. **Golden Ratio (:math:`\phi`)**: Algebraic, radical, continued-fraction, power-linearization, and pentagonal trigonometric/spread identities.
2. **Fibonacci & Lucas Sequences**: Binet, Cassini, d'Ocagne, Catalan, Vajda, summation laws, Millin series, and Pythagorean triple generators.
3. **Triangle Centers, Areas & Circles**: Heron, inradius, circumradius, exradii, Euler's :math:`OI^2 = R(R-2r)`, Nine-Point Circle, medians, angle bisectors, and barycentric/trilinear coordinates cross-linked to ``geometor/etc``.
4. **Classical Geometric Theorems**: Ceva, Menelaus, Stewart, Ptolemy, Brahmagupta, Parameshvara, Morley, Kepler's triangle, and Golden Cuboid / Platonic solid invariants.
