POS-1.08 · Unit Square Diagonal Circle & Equilateral Hybrid Locus
=================================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(sqrt(2), sqrt(3), phi)` | **Classification:** **Net-New** | **Concordance:** Quadratic circle–circle radical locus

.. enunciation::

   Erect equilateral triangle :g:`ABE` on side :g:`AB` of unit square :g:`ABCD`. A circle :g:`cE` centered at :g:`E` with radius equal to square diagonal :g:`AC` (:math:`\sqrt{2}`) cuts ray :g:`AB` at :g:`F` so that :math:`|AB|/|BF| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct unit square :g:`ABCD` with diagonal :g:`AC` of length :math:`\sqrt{2}`.

.. step::

   Erect equilateral triangle :g:`ABE` on :g:`AB` with apex :g:`E` :math:`=(1/2, \sqrt{3}/2)`.

.. step::

   Draw circle :g:`cE` centered at :g:`E` of radius :math:`\sqrt{2}`, cutting ray :g:`AB` beyond :g:`B` at :g:`F`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Any point :math:`(x, 0)` on :g:`cE` satisfies :math:`(x - 1/2)^2 + (\sqrt{3}/2)^2 = 2`, which simplifies to :math:`x^2 - x - 1 = 0`. Its positive root is :math:`x = \phi`, so :math:`|AB|=1` and :math:`|BF|=1/\phi`.
