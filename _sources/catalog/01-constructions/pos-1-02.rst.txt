POS-1.02 · 1:2:sqrt(5) Right-Triangle Hypotenuse Arc Transfer
=============================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Equivalent** | **Concordance:** GC-01.02 · KNOTT-1.1 · HUNT-02.02 · :ref:`Euclid VI.30 <elements:vi.30>`

.. enunciation::

   In a :math:`1:2:\sqrt{5}` right triangle :g:`ABC` with right angle at :g:`B`, subtract leg :g:`BC` from hypotenuse :g:`AC` via circle :g:`cCB` at :g:`D`, and transfer :g:`AD` onto :g:`AB` at :g:`G` so that :math:`|AG|/|GB| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct right triangle :g:`ABC` with :g:`A` :math:`=(0,0)`, :g:`B` :math:`=(2,0)`, and :g:`C` :math:`=(2,1)`.

.. step::

   Draw circle :g:`cCB` centered at :g:`C` through :g:`B` (radius :math:`1`), cutting hypotenuse :g:`AC` at :g:`D`.

.. step::

   Draw circle :g:`cAD` centered at :g:`A` through :g:`D`, cutting base :g:`AB` at :g:`G`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Since :math:`|AC| = \sqrt{5}` and :math:`|CD| = 1`, :math:`|AD| = \sqrt{5} - 1`. Swinging :math:`|AD|` onto :math:`|AB| = 2` leaves :math:`|GB| = 3 - \sqrt{5}`, so :math:`|AG|/|GB| = (\sqrt{5}-1)/(3-\sqrt{5}) = \phi`.
