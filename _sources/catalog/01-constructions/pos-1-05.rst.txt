POS-1.05 · Unit Square Extended-Side Semicircle Through Opposite Vertex
=======================================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Variant** | **Concordance:** GC-01.01 · :ref:`Euclid III.31 <elements:iii.31>`, :ref:`VI.8 <elements:vi.8>`

.. enunciation::

   In unit square :g:`ABCD`, the semicircle centered at midpoint :g:`M` of :g:`AB` passing through :g:`C` and :g:`D` cuts ray :g:`AB` beyond :g:`B` at :g:`F` so that :math:`|AB|/|BF| = \phi`.

.. picture:: pos-1-05.svg
   :alt: POS-1.05 — Unit Square Extended-Side Semicircle Through Opposite Vertex

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct unit square :g:`ABCD` with :g:`A` :math:`=(0,0)`, :g:`B` :math:`=(1,0)`, :g:`C` :math:`=(1,1)`, :g:`D` :math:`=(0,1)`.

.. step::

   Bisect :g:`AB` at :g:`M` :math:`=(1/2, 0)` and draw circle :g:`cMC` centered at :g:`M` through :g:`C`.

.. step::

   Intersect :g:`cMC` with ray :g:`AB` beyond :g:`B` at :g:`F`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Radius :math:`|MC| = \sqrt{(1/2)^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5}/2`, so :math:`|AF| = (1+\sqrt{5})/2 = \phi` and :math:`|BF| = (\sqrt{5}-1)/2 = 1/\phi`, giving :math:`|AB|/|BF| = \phi`.
