POS-1.01 · Classical Square Side-Midpoint Arc Extension
=======================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Equivalent** | **Concordance:** GC-01.01 · KNOTT-1.1 · HUNT-02.01 · :ref:`Euclid II.11 <elements:ii.11>`

.. enunciation::

   Given segment :g:`AB`, erect square :g:`ABCD`, bisect side :g:`AD` at :g:`M`, transfer :g:`MB` onto ray :g:`DA` beyond :g:`A` at :g:`E`, and swing :g:`AE` onto :g:`AB` at :g:`G` so that :math:`|AG|/|GB| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Given :g:`A` :math:`=(0,0)` and :g:`B` :math:`=(2,0)`, erect square :g:`ABCD` with :g:`C` :math:`=(2,2)` and :g:`D` :math:`=(0,2)`.

.. step::

   Bisect :g:`AD` at :g:`M` :math:`=(0,1)` and draw circle :g:`cMB` centered at :g:`M` through :g:`B`.

.. step::

   Intersect :g:`cMB` with line :g:`AD` below :g:`A` at :g:`E` :math:`=(0, 1-\sqrt{5})`.

.. step::

   Draw circle :g:`cAE` centered at :g:`A` through :g:`E`, cutting :g:`AB` at :g:`G` :math:`=(\sqrt{5}-1, 0)`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Right triangle :math:`\triangle MAB` has legs :math:`1` and :math:`2`, giving hypotenuse :math:`|MB| = \sqrt{5}`. Thus :math:`|AG| = |AE| = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi` and :math:`|GB| = 3 - \sqrt{5} = 2/\phi^2`, yielding :math:`|AG|/|GB| = \phi`.
