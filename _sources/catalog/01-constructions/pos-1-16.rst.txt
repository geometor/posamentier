POS-1.16 · Mascheroni / Hofstetter Compass-Only Circle Construction
===================================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Equivalent** | **Concordance:** GC-01.05 · :person:`Kurt Hofstetter <hofstetter>`

.. enunciation::

   Using strictly five compass circles and zero straightedge lines, Hofstetter constructs :g:`X` collinear with :g:`C` and :g:`D` such that :math:`|CD|/|DX| = \phi`.

.. picture:: pos-1-16.svg
   :alt: POS-1.16 — Mascheroni / Hofstetter Compass-Only Circle Construction

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Draw unit circles :g:`c1` centered at :g:`A` :math:`=(0,0)` through :g:`B` :math:`=(1,0)` and :g:`c2` centered at :g:`B` through :g:`A`, intersecting at :g:`C` and :g:`D`.

.. step::

   Draw :g:`c3` centered at :g:`C` through :g:`D`, meeting :g:`c1` at :g:`E` and :g:`c2` at :g:`F`.

.. step::

   Draw :g:`c4` centered at :g:`B` through :g:`E` and :g:`c5` centered at :g:`A` through :g:`F`, intersecting below :g:`AB` at :g:`X`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

With :math:`|CD| = \sqrt{3}` and radii :math:`|BE| = |AF| = 2`, :math:`X = (1/2, -\sqrt{15}/2)`. Hence :math:`|DX| = (\sqrt{15}-\sqrt{3})/2 = \sqrt{3}/\phi`, yielding :math:`|CD|/|DX| = \phi`.
