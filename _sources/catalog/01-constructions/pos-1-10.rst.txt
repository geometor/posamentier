POS-1.10 · Huntley's 3-4-5 Right-Triangle Angle Bisector Circle
===============================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Equivalent** | **Concordance:** GC-04.01 · HUNT-03.01 · :person:`H. E. Huntley <huntley>` · :ref:`Euclid VI.3 <elements:vi.3>`

.. enunciation::

   In a :math:`3\text{–}4\text{–}5` right triangle :g:`ABC` (:math:`|AC|=3, |AB|=4`), the angle bisector of :g:`C` meets :g:`AB` at :g:`E`. Circle :g:`cE` through :g:`A` cuts line :g:`CE` at :g:`P` and :g:`Q` so that :math:`|PQ|/|CP| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct :math:`3\text{–}4\text{–}5` right triangle :g:`ABC` with :g:`A` :math:`=(0,0)`, :g:`B` :math:`=(4,0)`, :g:`C` :math:`=(0,3)`.

.. step::

   Draw the angle bisector of :g:`C`, meeting :g:`AB` at :g:`E` :math:`=(3/2, 0)`.

.. step::

   Draw circle :g:`cE` centered at :g:`E` through :g:`A`, cutting line :g:`CE` at :g:`P` and :g:`Q`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

By :ref:`Euclid VI.3 <elements:vi.3>`, :math:`|AE|/|EB| = 3/5`, so :math:`|AE| = 3/2` and :math:`|CE| = \frac{3}{2}\sqrt{5}`. Thus :math:`|CP| = \frac{3}{2}(\sqrt{5}-1) = 3/\phi` and diameter :math:`|PQ| = 3`, giving :math:`|PQ|/|CP| = \phi`.
