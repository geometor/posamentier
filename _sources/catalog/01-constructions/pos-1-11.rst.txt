POS-1.11 · Three Concentric Circles (r = 1, 2, 4) Secant-Tangent Transversal
============================================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Variant** | **Concordance:** GC-06.02 · Power-of-point radial scaling

.. enunciation::

   Three concentric circles :g:`c1`, :g:`c2`, :g:`c3` have radii :math:`1, 2, 4`. A tangent to :g:`c1` at :g:`T` cuts :g:`c2` at :g:`A, B` and :g:`c3` at :g:`D` so that :math:`|AB|/|BD| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct concentric circles :g:`c1`, :g:`c2`, :g:`c3` centered at :g:`O` :math:`=(0,0)` with radii :math:`1, 2, 4`.

.. step::

   Draw horizontal tangent :math:`y=1` to :g:`c1` at :g:`T` :math:`=(0,1)`.

.. step::

   Mark intersections :g:`A, B` with :g:`c2` and :g:`D` (rightward) with :g:`c3`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Pythagoras gives :math:`|TB| = \sqrt{2^2 - 1^2} = \sqrt{3}` and :math:`|TD| = \sqrt{4^2 - 1^2} = \sqrt{15}`. Hence :math:`|AB| = 2\sqrt{3}` and :math:`|BD| = \sqrt{3}(\sqrt{5}-1) = 2\sqrt{3}/\phi`, so :math:`|AB|/|BD| = \phi`.
