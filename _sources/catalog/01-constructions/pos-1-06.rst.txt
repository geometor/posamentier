POS-1.06 · George Odom's Inscribed Equilateral Triangle Midline Chord
=====================================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(sqrt(3), phi)` | **Classification:** **Equivalent** | **Concordance:** GC-02.01 · :ref:`Euclid III.35 <elements:iii.35>` · :person:`George Odom <odom>`

.. enunciation::

   In equilateral triangle :g:`ABC` inscribed in circle :g:`cO`, the line through midpoints :g:`M` and :g:`N` of :g:`AB` and :g:`AC` meets :g:`cO` at :g:`P` so that :math:`|MN|/|NP| = \phi`.

.. picture:: pos-1-06.svg
   :alt: POS-1.06 — George Odom's Inscribed Equilateral Triangle Midline Chord

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Inscribe equilateral :g:`ABC` in circle :g:`cO` centered at :g:`O` :math:`=(0,0)` of radius :math:`2`.

.. step::

   Mark midpoints :g:`M` of :g:`AB` and :g:`N` of :g:`AC`.

.. step::

   Extend chord :g:`MN` to meet circle :g:`cO` at :g:`P` beyond :g:`N`.

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

By :ref:`Intersecting Chords (Euclid III.35) <elements:iii.35>` on :g:`AC` and :g:`MP` at :g:`N`, :math:`|MN|\cdot|NP| = |AN|\cdot|NC|`. With :math:`|MN|=|AN|=|NC|=\sqrt{3}`, setting :math:`x = |NP|` gives :math:`x(x + \sqrt{3}) = 3`, so :math:`|MN|/|NP| = \phi`.
