POS-1.14 · Hans Walser's Coordinate-Grid Radical Circles (r = 1 and r = 3)
==========================================================================

**Field:** :math:`Q(phi)` | **Classification:** **Net-New** | **Concordance:** :person:`Hans Walser <walser>` · :cite:`walser-2001`

.. enunciation::

   On an integer coordinate grid, circle :g:`c1` centered at :g:`O1` :math:`=(0,0)` of radius :math:`1` and circle :g:`c2` centered at :g:`O2` :math:`=(2,1)` of radius :math:`3` define a center-line :g:`O1O2` cutting :g:`c1` at :g:`A` and :g:`B` such that :math:`|AB|/|BO2| = \phi`.

Constructive Steps
------------------

.. step::

   Construct circle :g:`c1` centered at :g:`O1` :math:`=(0,0)` with radius :math:`1`.

.. step::

   Construct circle :g:`c2` centered at :g:`O2` :math:`=(2,1)` with radius :math:`3`.

.. step::

   Draw center-line :g:`O1O2`, cutting :g:`c1` at :g:`A` (far) and :g:`B` (near :g:`O2`).

Exact Symbolic Proof & Certificate
----------------------------------

Since :math:`|O1O2| = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5}` and :math:`|AB| = 2`, :math:`|BO2| = \sqrt{5} - 1 = 2/\phi`, giving :math:`|AB|/|BO2| = \phi`.
